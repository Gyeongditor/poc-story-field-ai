The four dragons - the blue dragon, the red dragon, the white dragon, and the black dragon - loved to enjoy adventure. They with different abilities, the blue dragon mastered water, the red dragon controlled flames, the white dragon handled ice, and the black dragon controlled the wind. They could go anywhere with their respective abilities and see anything. One day, they decided to explore Baekdu Mountain, a beautiful mountain in a distant land.

2Page:
When the dragons decided to go to Baekdu Mountain, they called up a huge dragon boat and boarded it. The huge dragon boat was big enough to carry 120 tourists and could go anywhere they wanted. On the huge boat, the blue dragon had fun drinking water, the red dragon teasing flames, the white dragon playing with ice pieces, and the black dragon had fun blowing wind.

3Page:
When the dragons arrived at Mt. Baekdu, they admired the beautiful scenery and peaceful atmosphere. They explored the lush forests of Mt. Baekdu, crossed the refreshing rivers, and enjoyed the scenery stretching far from the high peaks. The experience remained a precious memory for them, and they promised to visit there again.

The next destination for the dragons was Siberia. They decided to go all the way to Mongolia by train. The train was in poor condition, but it was just as impressive a trip. The train was supposed to travel at night and then again when we arrived in the morning. This experience gave them new perspectives and senses.

The dragon's journey is not over. They were planning to see more of the world. They chose Japan as their next destination and decided to enjoy various experiences there.
