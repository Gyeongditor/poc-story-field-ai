Once upon a time, there lived a girl in a small village who always dreamed of traveling. She wanted to go there while looking at the clouds floating high in the sky and touching the mysterious creatures in the water while looking at the blue sea. But the villagers always broke their dreams by telling her, "Travel is dangerous!"

[PAGE]  
One day, she mustered up her courage and decided to go on a journey to achieve her dream. She opened the door with food, maps, and a travel journal she had written so far in her precious suitcase. "This is the beginning!" One left the town with a pounding heart. She decided that her first stop was the top of a windy mountain.

[PAGE]  
Upon arriving at the top of the mountain, Hana tried to touch the cloud with her hands, but it darted out through her fingers. Then, a mysterious fairy appeared and gave her wings, saying, "You can't touch it, but you can fly in it!" One soared into the clouds, and looked down at the village and shouted, "Now my dream has come true!"

[PAGE]  
When Hana returned to town after the trip, the crowd listened carefully to her story. Hana talked about the joys of traveling and beautiful scenery, and encouraged people to travel. People were wary at first, but they became attracted to one passionate story and began to want to travel one by one. 

[PAGE]  
In the end, the villagers learned the joy of traveling through a story and decided to go on a new adventure together. Now the town is crowded with people going on vacation. And the first became a travel evangelist who helps them achieve their dreams, not only for themselves but also for many people. That's how the town became full of the joy of traveling.
